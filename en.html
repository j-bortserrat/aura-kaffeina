The place

A slow brunch,
a coffee that stands out.

AURA was born in Ruzafa as a place to pause. We slow-roast conversation and serve specialty coffee alongside a brunch designed to start —or stretch— the day.

Local produce, sourdough, seasonal fruit and a team that treats every dish like the first one.
